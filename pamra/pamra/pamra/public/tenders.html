How to participate

Bidding documents may be downloaded above or collected from PAMRA HQ during office hours. All tenders are also published on the PPRA portal. Sealed bids must reach the Procurement Section before the closing date and time stated in the tender notice.

For procurement queries: procurement@pamra.punjab.gov.pk · +92-42-99231400. PAMRA reserves the right to accept or reject any or all bids under PPRA Rule 35.
