Integrity Ledger

The tamper-evidence layer. Sensor-captured truck facts — number plate and net weight — are sealed into an append-only, hash-chained record. There is no edit button: any change to a sealed value breaks the chain and is detected instantly. Manipulation is detected and deterred.

How the Integrity Ledger works

1

Capture

The gate reads the truck's number plate with a camera (ANPR) and weighs it twice for net weight. Nothing is typed by hand.

2

Seal

Each truck's facts are locked into a block and stamped with a unique digital fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash).

3

Chain

Every block also stores the previous block's fingerprint, linking all blocks together like a chain.

4

Verify

Change any sealed value and its fingerprint stops matching, so tampering is detected the instant the chain is checked.
