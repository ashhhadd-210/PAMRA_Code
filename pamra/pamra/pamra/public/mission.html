PAMRA Act XII of 2018

Our Mission & Mandate

PAMRA exists to make Punjab's agricultural markets transparent, fair and accountable — putting growers at the centre of every transaction.

Mission

To regulate and modernise Punjab's agricultural markets through verified, attributable data — breaking information asymmetry so growers receive a fair price and the state earns the right to forecast.

Vision

A digitally integrated market ecosystem with end-to-end supply-chain visibility, transparent price monitoring and real-time analytics for evidence-based policymaking and market efficiency.

Core values

What guides every decision.

I

Integrity first

Every figure must be verified, attributable and tamper-resistant before it informs a decision.

G

Growers first

The PAMRA Act places growers at the legislative centre. Closing the farmer–market distance is our purpose.

T

Transparency

Open price discovery and public reporting protect farmers and the public from manipulation.

E

Evidence-based

Policy runs on clean, timely data — not estimates, anecdotes or partial samples.

S

Data sovereignty

Commodity data runs on PAMRA-owned, on-premise infrastructure and never leaves the department's control.

A

Accountability

A named owner and timestamp behind every record — anomalies have an owner, not just a colour on a chart.

Statutory mandate

What the PAMRA Act 2018 empowers us to do.

Regulate markets

License, register and supervise auction yards, wholesale markets and committee operations across Punjab.

Fair auctions

Promote transparent, competitive auction systems and improve transaction transparency.

Market information

Provide market information services — live prices, arrivals and trends — to all stakeholders.

Alternative channels

Promote alternative marketing channels and support contract farming.

Grower support

Deliver meaningful support to growers — digital receipts, payment visibility and market history.

Infrastructure

Upgrade and maintain market infrastructure — weighbridges, auction sheds, cold storage and CCTV.

Integrity before intelligence.

Our mandate is delivered through the Unified Market Platform and the PATMN trust infrastructure — register the farmer, verify every truck, lock data entry, then forecast.
