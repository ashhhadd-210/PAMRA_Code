About PAMRA

The Punjab Agricultural Marketing Regulatory Authority is the statutory body responsible for transforming, regulating, and modernizing agricultural produce markets across Punjab, Pakistan.

Our Mandate

PAMRA was established under the Punjab Agricultural Marketing Regulatory Authority Act 2018. Its core mandate covers:

  • Regulation: License and supervise agricultural produce markets and auction yards.
  • Modernization: Upgrade physical infrastructure and adopt digital systems.
  • Transparency: Ensure fair price discovery through open, monitored auctions.
  • Farmer welfare: Protect cultivators from middleman exploitation and undervaluation.
  • Private sector engagement: Encourage investment in market infrastructure.
  • Data integrity: Maintain accurate market data for evidence-based policy.

History

2018

PAMRA Act enacted by the Punjab Assembly, establishing the Authority and dissolving the old market committee model in favor of a centralized regulator.

2019

Initial pilot of MCMIS (Market Committee Management Information System) across 10 markets in central Punjab.

2020

Launch of AMIS (amis.pk) — public commodity price portal updated daily.

2022

Launch of Mandi Auction App (mandiauction.punjab.gov.pk) for live in-mandi auction capture.

2026

Rollout of the Unified Market Intelligence Platform — integrating MCMIS, AMIS, and Mandi App with AI-driven gate automation and a four-layer data authenticity engine.

Legal foundation

PAMRA Act 2018

Punjab Act No. XII of 2018 — empowering the Authority to regulate agricultural produce markets.

Did you know?

Punjab is Pakistan's largest agricultural province, contributing over 65% of national food grain output. PAMRA regulates the markets where over Rs 18 crore of produce changes hands every month.
