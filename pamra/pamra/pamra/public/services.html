Services

PAMRA provides regulatory, operational, and digital services to farmers, traders, market committees, and government policymakers.

F

For Farmers

Protecting cultivators from undervaluation through transparent price discovery and fair auction practices.

  • Daily commodity prices published openly on prices.pamra.punjab.gov.pk — 30-day trends, modal/min/max per commodity per market.
  • Transparent auctions with live streaming to PAMRA HQ control room; reduce middleman manipulation.
  • Direct mandi access — registered farmers can transport produce to any PAMRA mandi without intermediary fees.
  • Grievance redressal via WhatsApp helpline and online complaint portal.
T

For Traders

CNIC-based registration, vehicle linking, and a digital trail of every transaction.

  • One-time online registration with CNIC verification via NADRA.
  • Vehicle/RFID linking — registered trucks pass through gate ANPR in seconds.
  • Personal auction history accessible 24/7 via OTP login.
  • License compliance tracking with automatic renewal reminders.

★

Unified Market Intelligence Platform

The digital backbone connecting every PAMRA system, every market, every transaction.

The Platform unifies MCMIS, AMIS, and the Mandi Auction App into one source of truth. Every incoming record is verified by a four-layer authenticity engine before being published.

LAYER 1

Cross-source validation

Records that disagree across MCMIS, AMIS, and Mandi App are flagged for review.

LAYER 2

Statistical anomaly

Prices and volumes outside the rolling 30-day distribution are caught automatically.

LAYER 3

Geo + staff

GPS must lie inside the market geofence; staff must be on the day's roster.

LAYER 4

ML fraud scoring

Trained model assigns a confidence score; low-score entries are quarantined for review.

G

Gate Automation

AI-powered capture at every mandi gate — no manual entry, no missing records, no fake transactions.

STATION 1

ANPR camera

Truck number plate read by camera, cross-checked against the registered vehicles database.

STATION 2

CNIC kiosk

Driver presents CNIC; NADRA-style verification confirms identity and links to plate.

STATION 3

Commodity AI

Overhead camera classifies the produce in the truck bed and compares with declared commodity.

STATION 4

Weighbridge

Load cells capture gross-in and tare-out weights automatically; net is computed and pushed to the records pipeline.

Every completed flow generates a fully verified arrival record in the Unified Data Bus — feeding directly into commodity dashboards, revenue calculations, and analytics.
