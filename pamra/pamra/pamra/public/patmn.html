Strategic Vision · Prepared for PAMRA Leadership

PATMN — Punjab Agricultural Trust & Market Network

Punjab's first trusted agricultural identity and transaction infrastructure, under the authority of PAMRA. Not a software system, a mandi app, or a digitisation project — the institutional layer that makes PAMRA the authoritative source of agricultural market intelligence for Punjab.

"NADRA became the trusted source of citizen identity. PAMRA can become the trusted source of agricultural identity and market intelligence."
1 · Jurisdictional clarity

PAMRA's mandate is not a constraint on this vision — it is its foundation.

PAMRA's authority, defined by the PAMRA Act 2018, covers Punjab's agricultural markets, market committees, licensed Arhtis and traders. PATMN does not try to identify every farmer in Pakistan — it identifies every participant who transacts within Punjab's market ecosystem, regardless of province of origin.

The Toll Network Principle

A farmer from Quetta shipping onions to Lahore doesn't fall under PAMRA's territorial authority. But the moment that consignment enters a Punjab mandi, PAMRA's transaction mandate activates — exactly like a toll network registering every vehicle at the point of entry, regardless of where it came from.

Designed around transactions, not geography

PAMRA is not regulating Balochistan. It is regulating activity occurring inside Punjab's agricultural markets — fully within its mandate.

2 · What the PAMRA Act 2018 enables

Broader than mandi management — and it puts growers at the centre.

Regulatory functions

Regulate agricultural markets · promote fair auction systems · improve transaction transparency.

Market development

Promote alternative marketing channels · support contract farming · provide market information services.

Grower support

Meaningful support to growers · digital receipts & payment visibility · market history access.

The legislation repeatedly uses the word growers — not Arhtis, not Market Committees, not Traders. The fact that farmers are currently distant from the transaction process is not a reason to exclude them. It is evidence of the transparency gap PAMRA was created to close.
