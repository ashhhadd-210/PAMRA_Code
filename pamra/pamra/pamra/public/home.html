The problem, quantified

₨85 of value is created between field and plate. The grower touches ₨5 of it.

The markup is protected by information asymmetry. A faster auction screen doesn't change who controls the number — it just records the same number more quickly.

Farmer
₨5/kg
Sells to the arthi at the farm gate. No price reference, no buyer choice.
Arthi · commission agent
₨25–35/kg
Aggregates, sets the rate, controls who bids. The information bottleneck.
Mandi · wholesale
₨35–55/kg
Auctioned and graded. Quantities recorded by a market operator.
Consumer
₨80–90/kg
Pays the full markup. Volatility is blamed on "supply."
Where the value leaks

Three failure points — one root cause: the number is never independently verified.

01 · Arthi intermediation

The commission agent is the only party who sees demand and sets the farm-gate price. The grower has no live reference and no alternative buyer.

IMPACT — Captures the grower's margin

02 · Unverified truck weight

Arrival weight is a single figure typed by one operator. With no empty-vehicle (tare) weigh-out, the real commodity quantity is unknowable.

IMPACT — Quantity can be understated at will

03 · Operator data manipulation

Market operators are paid to mis-record quantities. AMIS data is hand-keyed through a data-entry dashboard — exactly the surface that is gamed.

IMPACT — Corrupts every downstream figure

These are not three projects. They are the same missing control — a verified, attributable record of who brought what — appearing at three stages of the chain.

Root cause

Garbage in, garbage out.

The data PAMRA already collects fails four basic integrity tests — so no analytics layer built on top of it can be trusted.

Hand-keyed at source

Prices and arrivals are typed manually via the AMIS data-entry dashboard. The figure is only as honest as the operator.

The solution · architecture

One CRM-led platform, six modules — sequenced so integrity comes before intelligence.

1 · Integrity

Farmer & Stakeholder CRM

Register growers, arthis, traders and buyers. Every consignment ties to a real, attributable identity.

2 · Integrity

Net-Weight Capture

Two-weighment (loaded in / empty out) with weighbridge auto-feed. Quantity stops being a typed opinion.

3 · Integrity

Integrity Ledger

Dual-control entry, append-only audit trail and anomaly flags on operator inputs. Manipulation is deterred.

4 · Trust

Transparent Price Discovery

Live e-auction with the clearing price pushed to the farmer in Urdu, before they commit to a sale.

5 · Trust

Fee Recovery & Reconciliation

Market fees computed automatically from verified weights, closing the recovery leakage PAMRA worries about.

6 · Intelligence

Forecasting (earned)

Surplus / deficit and import-trigger models — switched on only once modules 1–3 produce clean data.

Cloud-prohibited by design: the platform runs on PAMRA-owned, on-premise infrastructure — no commodity data leaves the department's control.

Module 3 · Integrity Ledger — live

The number, independently verified.

The working prototype runs every record through a four-layer authenticity engine — cross-source validation, statistical anomaly detection, geo/staff verification and fraud scoring — writing each verdict to an append-only ledger. Tampering is flagged in real time and tied to a named user.

  • Dual-control entry — no single operator owns the number
  • Append-only audit trail — every figure traces to a person and timestamp
  • Anomaly flags on price drift, ghost arrivals and volume outliers
